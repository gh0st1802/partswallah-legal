Privacy Policy for PartsWallah

Effective Date: October 2, 2026

PartsWallah ("we," "our," or "us") operates the PartsWallah mobile application (the "App"), a marketplace platform that allows users to discover, list, buy, and sell automotive spare parts.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use PartsWallah.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use PartsWallah, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Password or authentication information
  • Profile information

Listing Information

When you create a spare-parts listing, we may collect:

  • Part name and description
  • Vehicle or compatibility information
  • Price and availability
  • Photographs and other uploaded media
  • Location or other information you choose to include in a listing

Transaction and Communication Information

If you use features that allow you to communicate or conduct business with other users, we may process information necessary to facilitate those interactions.

If payment functionality is provided through a third-party payment provider, payment information may be processed directly by that provider according to its own privacy policy.

Device and Usage Information

We may automatically receive certain technical information when you use the App, such as:

  • Device type and operating system
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Device identifiers where permitted
  • App usage and diagnostic information
  • Crash and performance information

The information collected may vary depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your PartsWallah account
  • Provide and operate marketplace features
  • Display and manage spare-parts listings
  • Allow users to discover and interact with listings
  • Facilitate communication between users
  • Process transactions where applicable
  • Provide customer support
  • Send important service-related notifications
  • Improve the App and its features
  • Monitor and troubleshoot technical issues
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, and security issues
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

We do not use personal information for purposes unrelated to the operation of PartsWallah unless permitted or required by applicable law.

3. Information You Make Public

PartsWallah is a marketplace. Information included in a listing may be visible to other users of the App.

For example, information such as a listing title, description, photographs, price, and other listing details may be displayed publicly or to other registered users.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in your listings or profile unless it is necessary.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information in the following circumstances:

With Other Users

Information that you voluntarily provide as part of a marketplace listing or user interaction may be visible to other PartsWallah users.

Service Providers

We may use third-party service providers to help operate the App, including services for hosting, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, payment processing, or other technical functions.

These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it according to applicable requirements.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable laws or legal processes
  • Respond to lawful requests from authorities
  • Protect the rights, safety, and property of PartsWallah, our users, or others
  • Detect or investigate fraud, security issues, or misuse of the App

We do not sell your personal information as a standalone product.

5. Third-Party Services

PartsWallah may use third-party services to provide certain functionality.

Depending on the version and features of the App, these services may include Firebase or other providers for functions such as authentication, database services, cloud storage, analytics, crash reporting, or notifications.

Third-party services may collect and process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer required, we may delete it or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your PartsWallah account and associated personal information by contacting us using the contact information provided below.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary to prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, or comply with legitimate business or legal requirements.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

Permissions are requested only when needed for the relevant functionality. You can manage permissions through your device settings.
